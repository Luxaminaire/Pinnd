Core Features:
The application should not require an internet connection to function.
Users are presented with a corkboard background, with visual interface elements necessary to create Pinns.
Pinns consist of a body text, a title, a background color, a size, and a position on the board.
When creating Pinns, users must write a title and a body text.
Users can edit the title & body text of previously-created Pinns.
Users can resize Pinns by dragging the corners or sides of the Pinn.
Users can move Pinns around the board by dragging the middle of the Pinn.
Users can delete previously-created notes.
When right-clicking, a drop-down menu appears that provides additional options.
A scroll bar or “read more” button appears on notes with too many lines of text.
Notes automatically save their position, text, and other attributes.
Users can change the color of the notes.
User must be able to download and access the application via web
System to move notes front/back

NFR-01: The app should load in 200 ms or less.
NFR-02: Auto-saves occur when the user closes the app, and every 30 seconds when the user is not typing.
NFR-03: Saving & loading boards should take less than 1 second in 90% of cases.
NFR-04: Movement of a widget should be within 3 pixels of where your mouse is.
NFR-05: The app has the capacity to handle up to 50 notes on the board per user.
